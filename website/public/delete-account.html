Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Virtual Mining Empire account and the data linked to it at any time.

Delete it in the app

Open the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to virtualminingempire1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to the app, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your farm, miners, mining history, coins, boosts, missions, streaks and referral data
  • Your leaderboard entry and notification token

What may be kept

Records of real-money purchases (amount, date and payment status) may be kept for up to 8 years where Indian tax and accounting law requires it. Anonymous, aggregated statistics that do not identify you may also be kept.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.
