Virtual Mining Empire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Virtual Mining Empire mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use it. If you have any questions, contact us at virtualminingempire1@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and email address those services share with us.
  • Game data: your farm, miners, mining sessions, coins, boosts, missions, streaks, referrals and leaderboard position.
  • Purchase records: what you bought, the amount and the payment status. Payments are handled by Google Play - card, UPI and bank details are entered with Google and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app version, crash reports and how you use app features.
  • Advertising identifier: the advertising ID of your device, used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • Notification token: a token that lets us send you push notifications, if you allow them.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and save your game progress.
  • To run the game: mining sessions, rewards, missions, leaderboards and referrals.
  • To process in-app purchases and deliver what you paid for.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch for in-game boosts.
  • To send notifications, for example when a mining session is ready to claim.
  • To fix crashes, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the app.

3. Third-party services

We use the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase - account login and database hosting (privacy policy)
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple - optional sign-in (Google, Apple)
  • Google AdMob - advertising (how Google uses data in ads)
  • Google Play Billing - in-app purchases (privacy policy)
  • Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Crashlytics - usage analytics, push notifications and crash reports (privacy policy)

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Rewarded ads are always optional and only give in-game items.

5. Virtual currency

Virtual Mining Empire is a simulation game. It does not mine real cryptocurrency or use your device's processor for mining. In-game coins and rewards have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time - see Delete your account. Some purchase records may be kept longer where required by law (for example tax rules).

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at virtualminingempire1@gmail.com to make a request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
